About Nikhitha Shree

The Voice of Pollachi

Nikhitha Sree is a talented musician and artist hailing from the beautiful town of Pollachi. With a voice that captivates and a passion for music that shines through every performance, she has become one of the most promising artists in the region.

Musical Journey

Starting her musical journey at a young age, Nikhitha has trained in both classical and contemporary music styles. Her versatility allows her to perform across various genres, from traditional Tamil songs to modern hits.
